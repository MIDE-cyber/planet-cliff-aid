# PlanetCliffAid

PlanetCliffAid is a blockchain-based social impact platform that creates transparent, incentive-aligned ecosystems for measuring and accelerating community-driven environmental and social initiatives. The platform introduces Impact Proof Tokens (IPTs) - dynamic NFTs that evolve and appreciate in value based on verified real-world outcomes rather than speculation. Each community initiative begins as a Catalyst Contract with specific, measurable goals verified through IoT sensors, satellite imagery, and community reporting.

The technical innovation centers around the Outcome Oracle Network, which aggregates data from multiple verification sources including environmental sensors, third-party APIs, community validators, and machine learning models to create tamper-resistant impact scores. Smart contracts automatically distribute funding based on milestone achievements, featuring a unique retroactive funding mechanism that rewards initiatives showing the highest long-term impact after 12-month evaluation periods. The platform operates through Community Impact DAOs where local stakeholders vote on priority initiatives and validate outcomes through a reputation-weighted consensus system.

PlanetCliffAid addresses the critical gap between impact investment intentions and measurable outcomes while creating sustainable funding loops that reward genuine community transformation over mere activity metrics. The platform generates revenue through transaction fees, premium analytics for impact investors, and a marketplace for trading outcome-based carbon credits and social impact certificates. Its novel Ripple Effect Algorithm tracks secondary impacts across interconnected communities, creating bonus rewards for initiatives that generate positive spillover effects beyond their immediate scope.
